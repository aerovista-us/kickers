01 / The Opportunity

LET'S MAKE
THE NIGHT MEMORABLE.

Kickers has an opportunity to create a true local tradition: a place where the regulars feel at home, first-timers feel welcome, and the room comes alive when the band finds its groove.

The former picture-house setting offers a powerful story. Once, people came to watch the show. Now, they can become part of it. That idea can connect the music, the visuals, the dance floor, and the way Kickers introduces itself to the community.

The creative spark

“Used to watch the stories up on that screen. Now we're making memories in blue jeans.”

A proposed lyric inspired by the building's next chapter.

MODERN WESTERN.
VINTAGE SOUL.

03 / The Signature Sound

A SONG THAT
MOVES THE ROOM.

Our proposed original anthem, “Turn the Room Around,” blends honky-tonk character with a fresh country-boogie pulse.

A lively bass line. A distinct guitar motif. A double heel-stomp. Playful vocals that sound like two people enjoying themselves. And a chorus that lives just as comfortably in the room as it does in a video.

04 / The Dance

GIVE THE FLOOR
ITS OWN MOVE.

Imagine a few regulars recognizing the opening notes. Then a few more join. Someone teaches a newcomer the steps. Before long, it's a tradition people look forward to sharing.

The proposed Kickers Turnaround is an approachable original line-dance concept, designed around a signature back-and-forward movement and a quarter-turn. We would develop it alongside a dance instructor, then film a clear tutorial so everyone has a way in.

07 / Our Approach

BUILT WITH KICKERS.
NOT JUST FOR KICKERS.

Listen First

Learn what the owners, bartenders, DJs, dancers, and team want the venue to feel like. Confirm existing branding and priorities.

Make It Original

Develop several independent musical ideas, select the strongest, and refine it for both listening pleasure and a real dance floor.

Test With Real People

Put the song and steps in front of actual dancers and staff. Adjust the groove, mix, instruction, and timing before launch.

Measure What Matters

Look at dance participation, content engagement, attendance and feedback to learn what creates lasting value.
